Feed progress and predicted weight are computed from the EasyFeed FoxPro database (phase-specific mortality-adjusted head counts) and your Feed Budget's F/G ratios. Groups without a FoxPro match yet show blank feed columns. Feed/Head/Day uses a trailing ~10-day average from real bin-scale data (BinMaster/BarnTools/AP) where available; barns without bin-scale coverage yet show an "(est)" rate from the standard Feed Budget for that phase. Expected Close is that rate projected against the remaining Feed Budget. "Refresh Groups" re-pulls the doForms side (inventory, mortality, etc.) and re-matches it against the most recent FoxPro feed sync — it does not re-run the FoxPro sync itself, which still requires foxpro_sync.py on the mill computer.
